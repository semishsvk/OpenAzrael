# Modern controls

WASD moves, the mouse turns your view, Shift runs, and E interacts.

Tab switches between mouse look and the free cursor used for HUD buttons and inventory. Movement pauses while the free cursor is active. Esc opens or closes the service menu. H toggles the original help screen. F2 opens language settings. After switching away from the game, press Tab to resume mouse look.

This authorized input profile preserves production collision and interaction. It does not attest visual fidelity or replace original-runtime references.
